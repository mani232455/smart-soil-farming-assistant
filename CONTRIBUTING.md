# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in improving this prototype.

1. Open an issue describing the problem or proposed change.
2. Keep changes small and explain how they were tested.
3. Do not add fertilizer-dose or pesticide recommendations unless they are reviewed against reliable, region-specific agricultural guidance by qualified experts.
4. Never add personal farmer data, precise farm locations, or credentials to the repository.
5. Be clear about limitations and avoid unsupported impact claims.
